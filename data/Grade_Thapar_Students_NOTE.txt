Grade_Thapar_Students.csv — DISCLOSURE
=====================================
This file is a RELABELED copy of Grade_CS_Students.csv (335 real
students). The grade values are the original students' real grades;
only the column headers were renamed to Thapar B.E.(CSE) 2025 course
codes (see portal/thapar_map.py).

It is a demonstration stand-in dataset, NOT real Thapar student data.
Features: 22 core courses. Targets: 14 electives.
