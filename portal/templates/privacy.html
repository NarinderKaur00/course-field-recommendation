Privacy Policy

In the spirit of India's DPDP Act, 2023. This is a student project; adapt before any real-world use.

What we collect

  • Your name and email (to create your account).
  • Your subject marks (to generate elective/field recommendations).
  • Your course enrollments and progress.

Why we use it

  • Only to run the recommendation model and show your learning progress.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Your rights

  • Consent — we ask for it at sign-up; you can withdraw by deleting your account.
  • Access / portability — export all your data anytime (Settings → Export).
  • Erasure — delete your account and all data anytime (Settings → Delete).

Retention

Your data is kept only while your account exists and is removed immediately when you delete your account.
